Make them convert better (without overdoing it)

Use a mix of themes:

* 3 focused on privacy/discretion
* 3 focused on clarity/education
* 2 focused on professionalism/trust
* 2 focused on ease of using WhatsApp

That variety feels more authentic than ten saying the same thing.

Better than full names for credibility

Often stronger:

* Tunde A., Lagos
* Michael O., Abuja
* K., London

Initial + city can feel more believable than suspiciously polished full names.
